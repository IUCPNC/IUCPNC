## Academic access and employment categories

Academic Hub teaching functions are for currently appointed lecturers, including
management/administrative employees separately appointed to teach. Ordinary
employment, management or P&C status alone does not grant Hub access.

Approved student supervisors, external academic examiners, external industry
advisors and external academic advisors may receive narrowly scoped, dated
access to their assigned academic work. External examiners and external advisors
appear in the directory and academic profiles with an explicit **not an IUC
employee** distinction. Do not create employee status from an external appointment.

Part-time lecturers and part-time supervisors are IUC employees. Clearly
distinguish full-time and part-time employment in profiles and directory views;
reuse canonical employee/person identities. Follow applicable MQA/MOHE sources
and institutional appointment reviews; software validation is not compliance
certification. See `docs/ACADEMIC_HUB.md` for the implementation and rollout gates.
